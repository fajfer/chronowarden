# Release 0.7 Compliance evidence (extended scope)

- **Status:** in progress
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Issue:** milestone 0.7 Compliance evidence (#76, #78, #86, #24, #61 and #87-#96)

## Goal

0.7 produces evidence an auditor accepts, about the secrets **and** about Chronowarden itself. About secrets: systems
and owners, a hash-chained audit log with the change history of every secret, rotation confirmation, a privileged
flag, and frozen inventory snapshots on an opt-in cadence. About Chronowarden: every release is verifiable (SLSA Build
L3 provenance, cosign signature, CycloneDX and SPDX SBOMs) and the OpenSSF Scorecard goes from 6.8 to about 8.7.
Policies and PDF reports follow in 0.7.x; webhook alerting in 0.8.

## Non-goals

- Authentication and RBAC (0.9, ADR-015).
- Review state in Chronowarden (reviews happen in other tools; Chronowarden provides inventory and evidence).
- Webhook alerting and the scheduler (0.8, ADR-022, ADR-013).
- Writing other teams' metadata keys or Vault settings (ADR-004).
